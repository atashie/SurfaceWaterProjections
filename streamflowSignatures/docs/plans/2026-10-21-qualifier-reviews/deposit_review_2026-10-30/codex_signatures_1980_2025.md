## Checks performed (with the numbers obtained)

- **Inventory and schema:** all seven deposited files exist and are described. Every filename referenced by the README resolves either locally or to the correctly identified input companion. Summary: **6,250 unique gages × 2,277 columns**, comprising 20 metadata columns, 139×16 annual-statistic columns, 21 scalars and 12 flags. No missing statistic-pattern columns or duplicate dictionary/category keys.
- **Categories:** 139 annual signatures map exactly once: Flow Volume 23; Flow Duration 3; Storage 17; Flashiness 16; Drought 10; Flow Timing 15; Precipitation Streamflow 2; Snow 14; Climate 39. All deposited category/function-family mappings agree with the repository mapping. Dictionary and categories are **byte-identical to the sister product’s files**.
- **Annual census:** **34,813,881 rows**, **33,777,932 finite values**, **1,035,949 missing values**, no infinities, no duplicate `(gage_id, signature, water_year)` keys, no unexpected identifiers/signatures/years. All 6,250 gages and 139 signatures occur. Relative to the complete gage×signature×46-year grid, **5,148,619 rows are absent**, permitted by the documented missing-row convention.
- **Summary reproduction:** all **842,497 reported means** and **842,497 medians** reproduce within `rtol=atol=1e−9`. Qann annual counts and first/last valid years match summary metadata for every gage.
- **Changepoints:** all **836,721 reported changepoint years and Pettitt p-values** reproduce. All reported pre/post means and deltas reproduce; every reported split has at least ten valid observations on each side within WY1980–2024. Percentage changes reproduce using the absolute-denominator convention in finding 5.
- **Trend statistics:** checked all **772,739 finite full-series MK taus** and **772,739 finite MK p-values**; only the 14 elasticity cases in finding 3 fail. An additional stratified sample checked 174 reported trend-statistic values, exposing the same MK defect.
- **Identities and ranges:** seasonal totals match annual Q, precipitation and GDD for **255,145 / 250,296 / 250,296** complete gage-years. Q95−Q10 matches all **255,145** comparable spread values. Q/P matches **249,319** annual runoff ratios. All **6,222** reported runoff-ratio-high counts reproduce. No annual percentile-order, cumulative-flow-timing-order or precipitation-threshold-count-order inversions; tested annual bounds and integer count fields pass.
- **Daily climate reconstruction:** one projected pass over Daymet reproduced all **250,296 annual precipitation totals** and **250,296 GDD totals**, respecting record spans and interpolating Daymet’s omitted leap-calendar December 31. Maximum absolute differences were **9.1×10⁻¹² mm** and **1.4×10⁻¹¹ °C-days**. The stated climate-frame effect reproduces: **734 gages**, **952 valid gage-years**, **2,221 actual Daymet dates absent from streamflow**.
- **Flags and exceptions:** all 12 QA flags reproduce under the implemented definitions. `flagged_for_high_na`: **345 gages**, using **2,245 signature-derived columns**. Raw-flow gages: **28**, all Canadian, exactly matching missing drainage areas; **25** have the Qann-range flag. Their Q-to-precipitation outputs are NA, while climate and snow means remain available. `ice_affected_days_total` is zero for all 6,250 gages, as disclosed.
- **Correction ledger and frozen cohort:** ledger contains **193,542 restored days on 987 gages**; **119,933 days on 757 product gages** fall in this window. The companion lists **29 would-qualify gages**, none included.
- **Explorer:** embedded IDs exactly match summary order; all **2,245 signature-derived variables** are present. All embedded values and missingness agree at display precision. ZIP contains **139 signature JavaScript files**, totaling **237,153,193 uncompressed bytes**, under the documented folder. All **33,777,932 finite annual sidecar values** agree at display precision; no key, year-length or missingness mismatches. Dependencies are Leaflet from `unpkg.com`, Esri gray-canvas tiles and OSM fallback; **no active CARTO tile URL**. No wording implying an earlier public release was found.
