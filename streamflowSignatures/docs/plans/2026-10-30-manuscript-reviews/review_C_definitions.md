# Review C: deposited data-dictionary definitions vs SIGNATURES.md and the guidelines

## 1. Verdict

I read all 160 definitions against docs/SIGNATURES.md (whole file), docs/SIGNATURE_GUIDELINES.md (Parts 1-3) and config/signatures_config.json. **No definition states a wrong quantity, threshold, direction, unit or NA/0 rule: 0 HIGH findings.** Every threshold I could test agrees with the config: BFI parameters, 90/10 pulse percentiles, 2 % reversal threshold, 10 mm SWE threshold with "at or above", 60-day seasonal spell, 0.1 mm dry day, strictly-above 10/25/50 mm wet days, 5-day window, 0 degC frost, Oct-Jun frost-last window, GDD base 0, 11-year elasticity window, 10 mm / 1 mm runoff-ratio floors, drought percentiles 2/5/10/20/30 with strict "below". The dictionary is also right on the day-of-water-year origin and on zero-vs-NA for swe_max, swe_apr1, snow_on/off_dowy, swe_max_dowy and all the climate NA rules. About 81 of 160 signatures agree with both sources with nothing to add. The other ~79 carry at least one omission: 7 MEDIUM findings (rows M1-M7, touching roughly 65 signatures) and 8 LOW findings. The seven MEDIUM rows are all "a caveat the sources call essential is missing" (the NA rule of a family, un-normalized-gage NA, the Sep-Nov season origin, the avg_storage method). None is a contradiction.
