# Review B: Methods and Usage Notes vs code (blind)

Paths are relative to /Users/arik/github/SurfaceWaterProjections/streamflowSignatures. Line numbers in docs/MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT.md are given as "MS:n".

## Verdict

The three-stage QA description is the weakest part. Seven statements are WRONG:
1. The gap rule in 2.1.1 says the days of a long gap are "set to NA". The code rejects the whole water year.
2. The season definitions are shifted by one month from the code, the config and the guidelines (DJF/MAM/JJA/SON).
3. The "80 % of raw observations" rule is applied to annual metrics. Annual validity is actually at most 30 raw NAs (at least 91.8 % observed). The 80 % rule is seasonal only.
4. The Daymet description is wrong in tool, years and variables. The deposited basin series is exactextract with true-area weights, 1980–2025, 6 variables, 8,017 polygons. It is not gdptools, 1980–2023, with day length.
5. The 85,000 km2 statement is wrong. The only size cut in the code is 100,000 km2 (the polygon layer), and it is applied to polygons, not to the compile filter.
6. 2.1.2 says climate and land cover were aggregated "only where we had sufficient streamflow coverage". Daymet was aggregated for all 8,017 polygons.
7. "Flagged but not removed" overstates what users can see. The constant-value flag is computed but never exported. Negative flow is visible only as the `negative_ann` day-count signature, not as a per-year flag.

Several statements are IMPRECISE:
- The 60 % gage-inclusion denominator is the window start to the gage's last water year in the record, not the full window.
- The compile rule is per calendar year, with ≥ 30 days above 0.0001 in the active ingestion path. The manuscript says "> 30" and treats it as a gage-level perennial test.
- The 20-value floor blanks all 16 statistics, not only the trends.
- The 60 % and 80 % trend gates use the metric's own first-to-last valid year, not the analysis window.
- The Pettitt window and thresholds are not described anywhere in Methods.
- The USGS qualifier rule is not described.
- The Daymet-to-streamflow date join (climate frame) and the PPT/SWE/temperature QA policy are not described.
- The HydroATLAS description omits the primary rule: upstream-accumulated and pour-point attributes are taken as-is from the outlet sub-basin.
- "storage" is ambiguous in 6.1: only avg_storage and the Q–P family are NA. The baseflow and recession members of the Storage category stay valid.
- The record-dependent list in 6.3 omits the recession point-cloud alpha and the `_all` log(a) seasonality scalars.

Where guidelines and code disagree, both are reported:
- The annual 80 % rule (guidelines line 48 vs the ≤ 30 NA code rule).
- 85,000 km2 (guidelines line 28 vs the 100,000 km2 code constant).
